Contracting, Trading, Distribution & Stockholding

False Ceilings & Drywall

With years of experience in contracting, trading, distribution, and stockholding, Embroshyal Private Limited offers comprehensive false ceiling and drywall solutions for commercial, residential, and institutional spaces. The company works with leading industry brands such as Gyproc Saint-Gobain and Knauf Ceiling Solutions (formerly Armstrong), ensuring premium-quality materials and reliable performance across every project.

Our offerings include GI sections and channels, along with high-performance insulation materials such as rock wool and glass wool, designed to improve thermal efficiency, acoustics, and overall structural performance.

Embroshyal also specializes in advanced ceiling systems, including:

Baffle ceilings
Open cell ceilings
Decorative acoustic ceiling solutions

What services does Embroshyal Private Limited offer?

Where is Embroshyal based and what areas do you serve?
